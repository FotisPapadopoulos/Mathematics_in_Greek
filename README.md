# Mathematics in Greek — University Problem-Solving Archive

A comprehensive, curated academic repository of **550+ detailed handwritten step-by-step solutions, theorem proofs, and practical applications** from standard Greek university mathematics textbooks across **Calculus, Real Analysis, Linear Algebra, and Applied Statistics**.

---

### 🎯 Motivation & Philosophy
> *"Rigorous mathematics is the fundamental bedrock of intelligent computing and advanced engineering."*

This repository is born out of a genuine passion for pure and applied mathematics and a commitment to building a rock-solid mathematical foundation. Mastering core areas such as multi-variable calculus, rigorous analysis, linear algebra, and probability theory is essential for:
- **Artificial Intelligence & Deep Learning:** Understanding gradient optimization, high-dimensional vector spaces, tensor calculus, loss landscape geometry, and backpropagation foundations.
- **Data Science & Quantitative Modeling:** Formulating probabilistic models, statistical inference, matrix factorizations, and dimensional reduction techniques.
- **Algorithmic Problem Solving:** Developing formal logical thinking, proof construction skills, and algorithmic stamina.

Each directory in this archive represents a distinct university textbook, structured with dedicated chapter subdirectories, handwritten PDF solutions, and bilingual documentation.

### 🗂️ Systematic Indexing & Naming Conventions
All solution files follow a standardized, easily searchable nomenclature:

1. **Chapter & Page Based (`κ#_σ#_...`):**
   - Used in: `Calculus_I_Ntougias`, `Calculus_II_Ntougias`, `Applied_Statistics_Gnardellis`.
   - Pattern: `κ[Chapter]_σ[Page]_[Type/Number].pdf`
   - Examples: `κ1_σ45_α1.2.pdf` (Exercise 1.2), `κ2_σ85_θεωρημα_2.1.pdf` (Theorem 2.1 proof), `κ3_σ27_εφαρμογη_1.pdf` (In-text Application).

2. **Page Based (`σ#_...`):**
   - Used in: `Calculus_Spivak`, `Linear_Algebra_I_Korkotsidis`.
   - Pattern: `σ[Page]_[Type/Number].pdf`
   - Examples: `σ118_α10.pdf` (Page 118, Exercise 10), `σ170_θεωρημα_1.pdf` (Page 170, Theorem 1 proof), `σ175_πορισμα_1.pdf` (Page 175, Corollary 1).
